Marking countries

Click any country on the map to open it, then choose Visited, Lived or Wishlist. Turn on Quick Mark to mark with a single click (visited → lived → wishlist → clear), with Undo. Use Find a country to fly straight to any country. Scroll or use + and − to zoom, drag to pan, Esc to close a country.

Backup and restore

Settings → Export Backup saves every mark and year to a file. Import Backup merges a backup into this device without removing anything you already have.

Restore purchases

If you already subscribed or bought Lifetime Access, open the paywall or go to Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the Microsoft Store with the same Microsoft account used for the purchase.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Visit account.microsoft.com/services, sign in, and choose World Map: Countries Visited under Services & subscriptions.

Disputed territories

The App shows neutral, de-facto boundaries. Settings → Hide disputed territories removes contested territories from the map and from your statistics.

Country boundaries come from Natural Earth, a public-domain dataset. The App draws neutral, de-facto boundaries and takes no position on any territorial dispute.
