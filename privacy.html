Privacy Policy

World Map: Countries Visited for Windows · Last updated: 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how World Map: Countries Visited for Windows (the “App”), published by MD Shahariar Nazim Joy (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”), handles information. The App is private by design.

1. We do not collect personal data

The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. There are no accounts or logins. There is no analytics and no advertising.

2. Data stored on your device

The countries you mark (visited, lived, wishlist), first-visit years, your home country, target and preferences are stored only in the App's local storage on your Windows device. They never leave your device unless you choose to export a backup file yourself, and they are removed when you uninstall the App or choose Delete All My Data in Settings.

3. Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Microsoft through the Microsoft Store. We never see or receive payment card details. Microsoft's handling of purchase data is governed by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

4. Network use

The whole world map is bundled inside the App and it works offline. It connects to the internet only to download app updates, to let the Microsoft Store verify your purchases, and when you choose to open an external link in your browser.

5. No tracking, no location

We do not track you across apps or websites. The App does not request location, camera, microphone, or contacts access. Countries are marked only by you.

6. Children

The App is a general-audience travel journal. Because it collects no personal data at all, it does not collect personal data from children. Purchases are made through the Microsoft Store, where Microsoft family settings let parents approve or block purchases.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: dmjks120@gmail.com.

Country boundaries come from Natural Earth, a public-domain dataset. The App draws neutral, de-facto boundaries and takes no position on any territorial dispute.
